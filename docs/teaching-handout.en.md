# JenAI Studio — workshop handout

An AI studio for images and videos
that runs on your own machine.

## What it is

A small app that runs on your computer. You make images and videos with your own
key — no website in the middle, no subscription.

## Before the workshop

Install two things:
- **Node** — from https://nodejs.org (get the LTS version)
- **Git**

And sign up for one provider to get free credits:
- **Oxen.ai** — sign up at https://oxen.ai, then email hello@oxen.ai for $40.
- **Kie.ai** — sign up at https://kie.ai for 80 free credits.

Keep the API key handy.

## In the workshop

**First**, tell your Claude:

> Set up JenAI Studio for me.

Claude clones, installs, and opens the app at `http://localhost:4317`.
**You now have a website running on your own computer.**

## Paste a key

The popup asks you to pick a provider and paste your key — or skip and look
around. Your key stays on your machine.

## Make an image

Go to the **Images** tab, type what you want, hit **Generate**. It appears in the
gallery.

## The best part

Write a short brief, like:

> 10 photos of me as a chef in different cities.

Ask Claude to make them. Claude sends them to your local app, and the images
stream into the gallery one after another. Then ask for a video — same thing.

## If something breaks

You get a card with a plain explanation. Click **What do I do now?**, copy the
ready-made text, paste it into Claude, and keep going. No scary errors, no
running between laptops.
